# Channel: Cover letter

**What recruiters are tired of:**
- "I am writing to express my keen interest"
- "esteemed organization"
- "proven track record"
- "results-driven team player"
- "passionate about"
- "navigate complex challenges"
- "valuable addition to your dynamic team"

**What works instead:**
- **Open with the role and one concrete reason you fit:** a result, with a number.
- **Two or three specific things you did,** each with what changed because of it.
- **One honest line on why this company or role,** naming something real about them.
- **Plain, slightly formal, still human.** Contractions are fine; stock phrases are not.

**Length:** under 300 words. Never invent a result, a title or a date. Ask.
