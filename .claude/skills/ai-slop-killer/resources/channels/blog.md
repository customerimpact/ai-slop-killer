# Channel: Blog / article

**What readers are tired of:**
- "In today's fast-paced world" intros
- "In this comprehensive guide, we'll delve into…"
- Title Case Headings
- a bold phrase in every sentence
- a recap after each section
- "In conclusion"
- listicles of generic items

**What works instead:**
- **Answer first.** The first two sentences give the reader what the title promised.
- **Sentence-case headings** that say what the section says.
- **First-hand proof:** what the writer did, tested, saw or measured, with numbers, dates and screenshots where they exist.
- **Name sources** for any claim that isn't the writer's own experience.
- **End when the point is made.** A last useful step beats a summary.
- **Varied paragraphs.** Repeat the plain word rather than cycling synonyms.

**For SEO and AI-search content:** clarity and specific facts help both rankings and citations. Filler helps neither.
