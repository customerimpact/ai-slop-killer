# Channel: Newsletter

**What readers are tired of:**
- "Welcome back to another edition of…"
- "This week we're diving deep into…"
- "Here's the thing:"
- "Let's unpack this together"
- a sign-off summary of what they just read

**What works instead:**
- **Start with the story or the finding.** The reader opened it; skip the welcome.
- **One person writing to one reader:** "I" and "you", contractions, real opinions and small admissions.
- **Specifics from the writer's week:** what they tried, what broke, what it cost.
- **A short, plain next-issue line**, if any.

**Length:** whatever the point needs. Cut the warm-up first.
