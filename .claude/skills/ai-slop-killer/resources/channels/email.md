# Channel: Email (marketing, customer, announcement)

**What readers are tired of:**
- "Unlock your potential 🚀" subject lines
- "In today's landscape" openers
- "X isn't just another Y"
- feature bullets that fit any product (streamline, leverage, seamless)
- "take it to the next level"
- "Whether you're a startup or an enterprise"

**What works instead:**
- **A subject line someone can decide on:** the specific problem, offer or news.
- **The first line names one situation** this reader is in.
- **Bullets that say what changes** for this reader, in plain words, with a number where one exists.
- **One real proof point** (a customer, a result) before the ask.
- **One clear ask** that says what they get for clicking.
- **Signed by a person** when possible.
