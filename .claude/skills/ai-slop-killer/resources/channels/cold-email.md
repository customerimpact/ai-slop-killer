# Channel: Cold email

**What prospects are tired of:**
- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "I came across your profile and was truly impressed"
- "we empower teams to unlock…"
- "Whether you're X or Y"
- "quick 15-minute call?"
- exclamation-mark sign-offs

**What works instead:**
- **First line: one specific, true observation about them:** something they posted, shipped, hired for or said.
- **Then the one problem it suggests,** and what was done about it for someone like them, with a result.
- **An easy, specific next step** that is fine to decline.
- **Under 120 words,** plain text, no images.
- **Sounds like one person typing to one person.**

**Never invent** a mutual connection, a compliment about work nobody saw, or a client result.
