# Channel: LinkedIn

**What readers there are tired of:**
- the broetry layout (one line per paragraph)
- a hook, then three lessons, then "Agree?"
- humble-brag origin stories
- "I'm thrilled to announce"
- hashtag blocks
- 👇

**What works instead:**
- **Open with the specific thing:** what happened, with a number or a moment. One strong first line; no cliffhanger the post never pays off.
- **Paragraphs of two to four sentences,** with the occasional one-liner where it earns its place.
- **One clear point.** Lessons only if they come from what happened, and as many as there really are.
- **End on the point, or on one real question** only this writer would ask. Never "Thoughts?" or "Agree?".
- **Hashtags:** none, or two at most.

**Length:** 80–250 words for most posts.
