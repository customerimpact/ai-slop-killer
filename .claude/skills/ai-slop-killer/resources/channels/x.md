# Channel: X (Twitter)

**What readers there are tired of:**
- "Most people X. The top 1% Y."
- 🧵 thread hooks promising to change your life
- "Stop X. Start Y."
- "AI won't replace you, someone using AI will"
- numbered threads of platitudes

**What works instead:**
- **One concrete observation, result or opinion per post,** in the writer's natural register. Lowercase is fine if they write that way.
- **Threads:** the first post carries a real claim with a specific. Every post after it adds a fact, an example or a step.
- **No "here's why 👇".** Just say why.
- **Emoji:** one at most, and only if it's in the writer's voice.

**Length:** under 280 characters per post. Shorter is usually better.
