# Channel: Sales copy (pages, ads, funnels)

**What buyers tune out:**
- "Imagine a world where…"
- "revolutionize", "game-changing", "the solution you've been waiting for"
- "It's not just a tool, it's an ecosystem"
- ✅ bullets of generic benefits
- "join thousands of professionals"
- "look no further"

**What works instead:**
- **Open on the reader's specific situation,** in their own words where research exists.
- **Benefits as concrete changes** ("your Friday CRM update takes 5 minutes"), with numbers.
- **Real proof:** named customers, real results, screenshots.
- **Honest urgency only.**
- **One clear offer and one clear ask.**
