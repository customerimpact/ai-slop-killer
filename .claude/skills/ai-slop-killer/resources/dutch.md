# Dutch texts

Read this when the scanner reports `language: nl`. The scanner runs the Dutch rule set automatically, and the language-neutral checks (punctuation, emoji, rhythm, structure) run as well. Everything in `tells.md` still applies. This file adds what is specific to Dutch.

## Talking to the user

- Answer in the language the user writes to me in. If they write in Dutch, the whole report is in Dutch, including the pattern names ("omkering", "aankondiging", "opgeblazen belang").
- Quote their text exactly as written.

## Keep the writer's variety of Dutch

Find out from the text whether it is Flemish or Netherlands Dutch, and never switch it in a rewrite.

| Flemish (Belgium) | Netherlands |
|---|---|
| je kan, je wil | je kunt, je wilt |
| goesting, plezant, ambetant | zin, leuk, vervelend |
| dag, tot binnenkort | hoi, groet |
| kmo, zaakvoerder | mkb, ondernemer, eigenaar |
| gsm | mobiel, telefoon |

- Keep the writer's register: if they say "je", don't move to "u", and the other way round.
- Leave correct regional words alone. They are not slop.

## Dutch judgment tells (on top of the scanner)

- **Translated English.** Sentences that follow English word order or idiom: "dit gezegd zijnde", "aan het eind van de dag", "het maakt sense", "in de wereld van vandaag", "op een dagelijkse basis", "dat maakt zin". Fix: the Dutch expression.
- **Officialese in casual copy.** "middels", "teneinde", "dienaangaande", "inzake", "alsmede" in a blog or email. Fix: plain words ("met", "om", "over", "en").
- **English Title Case in headings** ("Hoe Je Meer Klanten Krijgt"). Dutch uses sentence case.
- **The "Vraag? Dan…" habit.** Once is natural Dutch. Five times in one article is a template. Turn most of them into "Als …, dan …" or a statement.
- **Bold lead-in on every bullet** ("**Snelheid:** …"), the Dutch versions of label sentences ("Het verdict:", "Vuistregel:", "De kern:").
- **Anglicisms where Dutch exists.** Keep terms of the trade the reader really uses (SEO, CRM, lead, funnel). Replace the ones with a normal Dutch word ("engagement" → "reacties", "lurken" → "meelezen", "verdubbel je op" → "zet je in op").

## Dismiss a Dutch scanner hit when

- **"Niet X, maar Y" corrects a belief the reader really has**, with evidence right after it. Dutch uses this construction far more than English, so judge each one; flag the pattern only when it repeats or when X is a claim nobody made.
- **"Bovendien" or "Daarnaast" joins two real points** in formal or academic writing.
- **"X, niet Y" is a plain instruction** ("Zeg 'Customer Impact', niet 'mijn bedrijf'"). Leave it.
- **A "Vraag? Dan…" sentence is a real instruction** in a how-to ("Lekt hij nog? Dan zit het gat elders.").

## Rewriting in Dutch

- No em dashes, as in English. Use a comma, a colon, a full stop or brackets.
- Dutch has no contractions to restore. Make it sound spoken through shorter sentences, normal word order and everyday words.
- Keep compounds written as one word (zoekmachineoptimalisatie, klantenservice). Don't split them English-style.
