# Deep audit

A line-by-line pass over the whole piece, for a writer who is about to spend money driving traffic to it or wants to learn why each part works or doesn't.

## How to run it

1. Build the Section Map first (`resources/conversion/modes/section-map.md`) but report it briefly.
2. Go section by section, in page order. For each one:
   - read its guide in `resources/conversion/sections/`;
   - score it 0–3;
   - mark the specific lines that help and the ones that cost readers.
3. Then judge the whole piece against each guide in `resources/conversion/craft/` and score each 0–3.
4. Close with the three changes that matter most, so the length doesn't bury the priorities.

## What to mark

- **Keep:** lines doing real work. Say briefly why, so the writer doesn't cut them in an edit.
- **Cut:** lines a buyer would skip, repeats, warm-up sentences, claims with no support.
- **Change:** lines with the right job done badly. Give the change and a suggested line.
- **Add:** what's missing at this exact point (a proof point after a big claim, a price anchor before the price, an answer to the doubt the line just raised).

## Report format

```
I read this as a [copy type] for [reader], asking them to [action]. [Couldn't read: …]

## [Piece name] · Copy Score: NN/100 ([band])
(Row table as in `resources/conversion/modes/five-wins.md`.)

**Section Map:** one line per section: name, score, and a few words.

## Section by section

### [n]. [Section name] · n/3
> "[quoted line]"
**Keep / Cut / Change / Add:** what and why. **Try:** "[suggested line]"

(Repeat for the lines that matter. Not every line needs a note; skip lines that are fine.)

## The whole piece
| Craft | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity | n/3 | … |
(one row per craft guide)

## If you change three things
1. …
2. …
3. …

## What I need from you
(facts for placeholders)

## Patterns readers notice
(See step 7 of the flow.)
```

Length follows the piece: a short page gets a short deep audit. Don't comment on a line just to fill space.
