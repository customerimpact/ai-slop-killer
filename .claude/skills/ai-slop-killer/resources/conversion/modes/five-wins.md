# 5 wins

The default conversion audit: the five changes that would sell the most, in order, for a writer who wants to know what to fix first.

## How to pick the five

1. Score the piece (see `resources/conversion/conversion.md`).
2. List every candidate change: each missing needed section, each section scored 0 or 1, and each craft problem.
3. Rank by **likely effect on sales × how many readers it reaches**. A weak headline reaches everyone; a weak FAQ answer reaches the few who scroll that far. Missing proof and an unclear offer usually beat style fixes.
4. Take the top five. Merge two candidates when one fix solves both. Never pad: if there are only three real wins, give three and say so.

## Report format

```
I read this as a [copy type] for [reader], asking them to [action]. [Couldn't read: … , only if something was missing.]

## [Piece name] · Copy Score: NN/100 ([band])

| Row | Score | What's holding it back |
|---|---|---|
| Clear offer | n/15 | … |
| Hook | n/15 | … |
| Reader fit | n/10 | … |
| Desire | n/10 | … |
| Proof | n/20 | … |
| Offer and price | n/10 | … |
| Doubts handled | n/10 | … |
| The ask | n/10 | … |

**What's working:** one or two lines, specific.

## The 5 wins

### 1. [The change, as an instruction]
**Now:** "[quoted line or a description of the missing section]"
**Why it costs you:** two or three sentences on what the reader thinks at this moment and why they leave.
**Do this:** the fix in plain steps.
**Try:** a suggested line or short block, with [placeholders] for facts I don't have.

(2–5 the same way.)

## What I need from you
Bullet list of the facts the placeholders ask for. Omit if none.

## Patterns readers notice
(See step 7 of the flow.)

Want the Section Map, a deep line-by-line audit or a rewrite next?
```

Keep each win under about 150 words. The whole report should fit on two screens.
