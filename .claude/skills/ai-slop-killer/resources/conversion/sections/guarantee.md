# Guarantee and risk reversal

**Its job:** move the risk from the buyer to the seller, so the reader feels that trying is safer than not trying.

**Needed on:** sales pages, product pages, checkout steps and webinar or VSL scripts, placed next to the price and again near the final ask. Trials and "cancel any time" do this job for subscriptions. Ads and emails can mention it in a line. Skip it only if there's truly no risk to reverse.

## What good looks like
- The terms are specific: how long, what counts, how to claim, how fast the refund comes.
- It's written in plain words a buyer would say, not legal boilerplate.
- It names the fear it's answering ("if it doesn't fit your workflow", "if you can't keep up").
- Any conditions are fair and stated up front, not buried in a link.
- It sounds confident, as if claims are rare and the seller is fine with that.

## What usually goes wrong
- "Satisfaction guaranteed" with no duration, process or meaning.
- Conditions so heavy (proof of work, every module done) that it's no guarantee at all.
- Hidden in tiny text below the button, so it never lowers the risk the reader feels.
- A promise the business can't honour, or one the checkout terms contradict.
- Defensive tone that hints refunds are a hassle.

## Scoring (0–3)
- **0:** missing where buyers carry real risk, or misleading (terms contradict the claim).
- **1:** present but weak: vague promise, no duration or process.
- **2:** does its job: clear period, simple claim process, fair conditions, near the price.
- **3:** a reason to buy on its own: the guarantee is specific to the buyer's fear and generous enough that saying no feels riskier than saying yes.

## Fixes to suggest
- State the period, what triggers a refund and how to ask for it, in two or three sentences.
- Name the exact worry it covers, in the reader's own words.
- Move it next to the price and the button, and repeat it once near the final ask.
- Remove conditions that exist only to block claims, or say them plainly.
- Check the wording matches the terms of sale word for word on duration and process.

## Example
**Weak (Ledgerly):**
> 100% satisfaction guaranteed. Terms apply.

**Stronger (Ledgerly):**
> Try Ledgerly free for 14 days, no card needed. If it doesn't save you time on your first invoice run, just don't subscribe. Your data exports to a spreadsheet in one click, so you never lose your records.

Why it works: it removes both risks a freelancer feels (paying for nothing and getting their books stuck in an app) with terms that are easy to check.

**Facts only the writer has:** the real guarantee or trial period, refund process and timing, conditions, and how often claims happen if that's shared.
