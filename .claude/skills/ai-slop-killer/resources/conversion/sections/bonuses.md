# Bonuses

**Its job:** remove a specific remaining hesitation, or speed up the first result, so the reader acts now instead of later.

**Needed on:** sales pages, webinar and VSL scripts, and launch emails, placed after the main offer and before or beside the price. Product pages rarely need them. Skip them entirely when nothing would genuinely help; a weak bonus does more harm than none.

## What good looks like
- Each bonus answers a known objection or obstacle ("I don't have the time", "I won't know where to start").
- It's something the buyer would use, ideally early, and its purpose is clear in one line.
- It's related to the main product and doesn't distract from it.
- If a bonus is time-limited, the limit is real and stated with a date.
- There are few of them. One strong bonus beats five weak ones.

## What usually goes wrong
- Filler: old PDFs, generic checklists, a "private community" with nothing in it.
- Bonuses that are worth more attention than the core product, which raises doubts about it.
- Invented values ("a €497 bonus") with nothing behind the figure.
- Fake deadlines on bonuses that never actually go away.
- Bonuses unrelated to the reader's goal, added because they were lying around.

## Scoring (0–3)
- **0:** padded or deceptive (fake values, fake deadlines) where trust matters.
- **1:** present but weak: generic extras that don't address any objection.
- **2:** does its job: relevant, useful, honestly described, tied to a real hesitation.
- **3:** a reason to buy on its own: one bonus kills the last objection so neatly that waiting no longer makes sense.

## Fixes to suggest
- List the top objections from sales calls or replies, then keep only bonuses that answer one of them.
- Write each bonus as "so you can…" followed by the result.
- Cut any bonus you wouldn't sell on its own or recommend to a friend.
- If a bonus expires, state the exact date and remove it when the date passes.
- Drop invented values. Describe the use instead.

## Example
**Weak (Harbor Roast):**
> Plus FREE bonuses worth €150: coffee e-book, brewing guide, exclusive members area!

**Stronger (Harbor Roast):**
> First delivery comes with a grind card: tell us your brewer and we'll grind to match, so your first cup tastes the way the beans should. Your choice is saved for every delivery after.

Why it works: it targets the real worry of a new subscriber (will this taste right at home?) and solves it at the first cup.

**Facts only the writer has:** the real bonuses, which objections they answer, any deadline and its date, and any value figure with its basis.
