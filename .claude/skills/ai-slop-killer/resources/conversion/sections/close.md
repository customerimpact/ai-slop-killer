# Close: the final section before the last ask

**Its job:** pull the whole case together in a few lines and put the decision in front of the reader, so the last ask feels like the obvious next step.

**Needed on:** sales pages, long landing pages, webinar and VSL scripts, and longer marketing emails, right before the final call to action. Short ads and product pages can skip it, letting the price and button do the work.

## What good looks like
- It recaps the core promise and the main result in one or two sentences, without repeating the page.
- It sets out the choice: stay where you are (with its real cost) or take the step (with what it gets you).
- It restates the key reassurance: price, guarantee or trial, deadline if real.
- The tone is calm and confident. It respects the reader's right to say no.
- It flows straight into the button, with no new topics.

## What usually goes wrong
- A summary that rehashes every section at length.
- New claims or bonuses introduced at the end, which raise fresh questions.
- Guilt or shame ("If you don't buy, you'll always regret it").
- Hype words that replace substance at the exact moment the reader is deciding.
- No close at all, so a long page just stops and the reader drifts off.

## Scoring (0–3)
- **0:** missing on a long page, or it pressures with guilt or false claims.
- **1:** present but weak: rambling recap, hype, or new topics that distract.
- **2:** does its job: short recap, clear choice, key reassurance, leads into the ask.
- **3:** a reason to buy on its own: the reader sees both futures plainly and in their own terms, and the ask lands as relief.

## Fixes to suggest
- Cut the close to five to eight lines: promise, the choice, the reassurance, the ask.
- Describe the cost of doing nothing with something concrete from earlier on the page.
- Remove any claim or bonus that appears for the first time here.
- Replace guilt with a fair statement of the two options.
- End on the exact action and what happens right after it.

## Example
**Weak (Ines Strength):**
> This is your moment! Don't let another year pass you by! Transform your body and your life today! Join now!

**Stronger (Ines Strength):**
> You can keep stretching your back at your desk and hoping it improves. Or you can spend three short sessions a week for 12 weeks, with Ines checking your form, and find out what your back feels like when it's strong. It's €390, and the next group starts on 12 January.

Why it works: it puts the two options side by side in the reader's daily terms and restates the facts they need, with no pressure.

**Facts only the writer has:** the core result buyers get, real session times and length, price, guarantee terms, start or close dates.
