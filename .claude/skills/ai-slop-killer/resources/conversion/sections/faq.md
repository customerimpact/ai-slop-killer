# FAQ

**Its job:** clear up the remaining practical questions quickly, so nothing small blocks the purchase.

**Needed on:** sales pages, product pages, pricing pages and landing pages, near the end, before or after the final ask. Funnel steps may carry a short version near the button. Ads and emails skip it. It doesn't replace handling the big objections in the body.

## What good looks like
- Questions are the ones buyers really ask (from support, sales calls, chat logs), in their wording.
- Answers lead with the direct answer (yes, no, a number), then add a line of detail.
- It covers the practical stuff: access, delivery, cancelling, refunds, payment, requirements, support.
- Answers match the terms, checkout and onboarding exactly.
- Each answer quietly sells where it can, without dodging the question.

## What usually goes wrong
- Invented questions that are really disguised benefit claims ("Why is this so amazing?").
- Long answers that bury the yes or no.
- Missing the questions that matter (cancelling, refunds, what happens after payment).
- Contradictions with the guarantee or pricing sections.
- So many questions that readers can't find theirs.

## Scoring (0–3)
- **0:** missing where practical doubts clearly exist, or it contradicts other parts of the page.
- **1:** present but weak: fake or vague questions, slow answers, practical gaps.
- **2:** does its job: real questions, direct answers, practical topics covered, consistent.
- **3:** a reason to buy on its own: answers are so clear and generous that the last doubts turn into confidence, and each one ends near the next step.

## Fixes to suggest
- Pull the questions from support and sales logs, and use the buyer's wording.
- Start every answer with the direct answer in the first few words.
- Make sure cancelling, refunds, access and payment each have an entry.
- Cut questions that are really sales claims, and move any real objection up into the body.
- Check each answer against the terms and checkout before publishing.

## Example
**Weak (Harbor Roast):**
> **Why is Harbor Roast the best coffee?** Because we're passionate about quality and care about every bean!

**Stronger (Harbor Roast):**
> **Can I skip a month?** Yes. Skip or pause from your account up to two days before your next roast date. Nothing is charged for a skipped delivery.
> **How do I cancel?** One click in your account, any time. No call, no form.

Why it works: real questions, the answer in the first word, and the details a cautious subscriber checks before signing up.

**Facts only the writer has:** real buyer questions, skip and cancel rules, cut-off times, refund and delivery terms.
